### Representative Case Studies

#### Dynamic Customer Rules Engine (Food Manufacturing)
**Problem:** Complex customer requirements causing $23K monthly in rework  
**Solution:** Hierarchical rules engine operating at customer/product/PO levels  
**Outcome:**
- 95% reduction in errors (9-12 monthly → nearly zero)
- $23K → $2K monthly rework costs
- 5-minute requirement changes (down from hours)
- Hundreds of last-minute changes handled without delays

**Key Innovation:** Granular rules that cascade from customer → product → individual PO, with real-time maintenance interface for in-flight orders.

#### Food Manufacturing ERP Integration (NetSuite)
**Problem:** NetSuite implementation requiring complete production workflow rebuild  
**Solution:** Bidirectional integration layer separating financial from production operations  
**Outcome:**
- 6-month NetSuite deployment with production continuity
- No retraining or process disruption
- Finance gained consolidated reporting
- Production kept specialized quality workflows

**Key Innovation:** Allowed NetSuite to handle financials while legacy systems handled production, with automatic synchronization between systems.

#### Process Control Alert System (Manufacturing)
**Problem:** Equipment failures averaging $45K per incident, 2-4 hour response times  
**Solution:** Real-time alert system integrated with process control network  
**Outcome:**
- 85% reduction in emergency escalations
- 8-12 monthly escalated failures → 1-2
- 6-8 issues addressed daily before escalation
- Reduced response times significantly

**Key Innovation:** Direct PLC/SCADA integration with intelligent alert routing and mobile access for maintenance teams.
