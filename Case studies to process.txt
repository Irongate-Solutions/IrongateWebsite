A customer would receive orders from a customer in 3-5 spreadsheets daily with upwards of 30,000 lines each day. We converted their process so that the emails containing the spreadsheets now get automatically received and processed. All orders are pulled from the spreadsheets, interpreted and converted as needed, sent to the manufacturing machines for production and imported into the company's billing system without any human interaction.
 
A world class food manufacturer receives dozens of tankers of milk daily. When the ODA determines any certain milk producer is no longer approved as top quality, processes that we put together will automatically detect that and refuse any deliveries from that supplier. Notifications are automatically sent to company management, alerting them of the issue.
 
A manufacturer provides specific customers with authorization to enter their secure building and put together their own loads even when no personnel are present. Our software prints RFID tags for all pallets and reads those tags during shipping as the driver loads his truck. The software automatically records all pallets loaded, prints a BOL and other shipping documents and imports the shipping information into the company's ERP system
